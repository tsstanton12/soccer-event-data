## Ball Interpolation Sweep Status

Interpolation settings were tested on existing Army and LeMoyne ball paths.

Findings:

- `max_speed=1800` and `max_speed=2500` made no difference on current paths.
- Increasing `max_gap_seconds` from `0.5` to `1.0` increased coverage but also
  increased possession segments.
- This warns that too much interpolation can invent continuity and ownership.

Reports:

- `outputs/ball_interpolation_sweep/army_smoke/interpolation_sweep_report.md`
